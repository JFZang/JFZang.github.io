Zeta Global: strong momentum, demanding per-share economics

Zeta is winning enterprise marketing budgets, but reported revenue and adjusted cash flow currently run ahead of gross-profit quality and cash earnings attributable to each share.

Investment view

The business is stronger than the stock setup. My base case already reaches management's 2028 revenue and company-FCF targets, yet values Zeta at $24.64 per share. At the current price, the reverse DCF requires a 22.3% terminal SBC-adjusted FCF margin—well above my 17.5% base.

Market expectations

The price requires more than hitting the 2028 plan

Zeta reported Q2 2026 revenue of $442.8m, up 44%, and management guides to approximately $1.818bn of FY2026 revenue, $405m of adjusted EBITDA and $255m of free cash flow. Its 2028 plan calls for at least $2.3bn of revenue, a 25% adjusted EBITDA margin and at least $371m of FCF.

I model $2.34bn of 2028 revenue and $377m of company FCF. The gap is treatment, not topline scepticism: after $140m of forecast stock compensation, SBC-adjusted FCF is $236m. The current price therefore appears to capitalise management's operating plan plus a rapid improvement in the portion of cash flow that actually accrues to current shareholders.

Core theses

Three claims that can be verified

01 · Revenue quality

Organic revenue is growing faster than organic gross profit

Q2 GAAP gross margin fell to 59.1% from 62.1%. Zeta's contracts can include third-party media, and its filing requires principal-versus-agent judgement. I need gross margin above roughly 61% or ex-M&A gross-profit growth to converge toward revenue growth before treating all incremental revenue like software.

02 · Per-share economics

Adjusted cash flow is not yet cash earnings per share

H1 2026 company FCF was $99.6m, versus $105.1m of stock compensation. Unrecognised SBC was $381.4m at June 2026, excluding future grants. My model assumes SBC falls from 11.0% of revenue in 2026 to 6.0% in 2028; failure to fall below 9% in 2027 is a material negative.

03 · Valuation

The operating targets are credible—and largely priced

My base forecast is not below management's 2028 plan. Upside requires a direct-mix shift, successful Marigold cross-sell and provable commercial returns from Athena and the Palantir partnership, alongside dilution discipline.

Valuation

SBC-adjusted FCF DCF, with a dilution-only FCFE cross-check

I use an equity DCF on SBC-adjusted FCF because EV/revenue is distorted by media pass-through and adjusted EBITDA excludes a material employee cost. The base case uses a 10.6% cost of equity, 3.0% terminal growth and 251.0m current common shares. A second path uses standard FCFE per forecast diluted share without deducting SBC; it returns $26.19 per share and avoids double counting.

Catalysts and decision rules

What would change the view

Potential catalysts

  • Q3 delivery against $470.5m revenue and $115.5m adjusted EBITDA guidance, with gross margin and direct mix as the real tests.
  • Zeta Live on 8 October 2026 and evidence of paying Athena or Palantir deployments.
  • Q4 ex-Marigold/political growth against the company's implied 20% path.
  • 2027 guidance, Marigold retention and cross-sell, and an inflection in net share issuance.

Evidence that would make me positive

  • Ex-M&A gross-profit growth above 22%, gross margin above 61% and direct mix above 73%.
  • SBC below 9% of revenue without slower growth or offsetting cash compensation.
  • SBC-adjusted FCF above a 7% margin earlier than 2028.
  • Repurchases exceeding all employee and acquisition issuance.

Thesis breakers

  • Q4 organic growth below 18%, two quarters below 58% gross margin, or widening gross-profit underperformance.
  • 2027 SBC at or above 10% of revenue, or diluted shares rising without matching SBC-adjusted FCF growth.
  • Material Marigold customer losses, impairment indicators, or established legal/regulatory failure.
  • Another equity-funded acquisition before per-share returns on Marigold and LiveIntent are demonstrated.
