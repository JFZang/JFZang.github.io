Background

Economics, finance and investment research

I studied Finance and Economics at Cambridge after completing a First Class degree in Economics at UCL. Work across audit, corporate finance, securities and banking sharpened my interest in how financial information becomes an investment decision.

I now use this site to develop that interest through company research, macro writing and empirical projects. My interests sit across company analysis, industry structure, macro conditions and empirical research, with valuation and risk as the connecting themes.
