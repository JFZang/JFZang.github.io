About

I studied Finance and Economics at Cambridge after completing a First Class degree in Economics at UCL. Across internships in audit, corporate finance, securities, and banking, I became increasingly interested in how businesses are assessed, how financial information is interpreted, and how investment decisions are made. This website is where I bring those interests together through written research and ongoing analysis.
